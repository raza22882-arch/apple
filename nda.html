For Access to Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), Proprietary, and Classified Data

1. Background and Purpose

The Receiving Party is being granted conditional access to certain confidential, proprietary, or legally protected information owned by, in the possession of, or under the control of the United States Government (the "Government"). 2. Definition of Protected Information

  • Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI): Information that requires safeguarding or dissemination controls pursuant to and consistent with applicable law, regulations, and government-wide policies.
  • Proprietary Information: Trade secrets, commercial, or financial information obtained from a person or business that is privileged or confidential (as defined under 18 U.S.C. § 1905).

3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

  1. Nondisclosure: I will not disclose, release, or transmit any Protected Information to any unauthorized person or entity, except as expressly authorized in writing.
  2. Safeguarding: I will protect all Protected Information from unauthorized disclosure by employing the physical and electronic safeguarding requirements specified by the Agency.
  3. Reporting: I will immediately report to the appropriate Agency Security Officer any unauthorized disclosure, suspected breach, or unauthorized attempt to access the Protected Information.

4. Mandatory Whistleblower Protection Enhancement Act (WPEA) Clause

Pursuant to U.S. Federal Law, the following provision is incorporated into this Agreement:

"These provisions are consistent with and do not supersede, conflict with, or otherwise alter the employee obligations, rights, or liabilities created by existing statute or Executive order relating to (1) classified information, (2) communications to Congress, (3) the reporting to an Inspector General of a violation of any law, rule, or regulation, or mismanagement, a gross waste of funds, an abuse of authority, or a substantial and specific danger to public health or safety, or (4) any other whistleblower protection."

5. Penalties for Unauthorized Disclosure

I understand that unauthorized disclosure of Protected Information may result in severe administrative, civil, and criminal penalties, including but not limited to fines and/or imprisonment under the Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. § 1905) and the Espionage Act (18 U.S.C. § 793, 794, 798).
